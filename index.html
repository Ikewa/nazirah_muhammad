Who We Are

About Binta Child Rights & Empowerment Foundation (BCRE)

Established in 2021 and officially registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) in 2025 (RC 8506854), Binta Child Rights & Empowerment Foundation (BCRE) is a non-governmental, nonprofit foundation dedicated to child rights, education, women’s empowerment, and youth development.

Rooted in Northern Nigeria with activities, advocacy, and partnerships extending to Abuja (FCT) and beyond, BCRE works alongside families, schools, traditional and community leaders, civil society networks, and development institutions to build just, inclusive, and resilient communities.

Meet Our Founder & Executive Director: Nazirah Muhammad

Lawyer • Social Development Practitioner • CBPR Researcher • Child Rights & Gender Advocate

Nazirah Muhammad is the Founder and Executive Director of Binta Child Rights & Empowerment Foundation (BCRE). Working at the intersection of law, community, and development, she brings together legal awareness, grass-roots community engagement, participatory research, and strategic partnership-building to protect children, empower women and girls, and mentor young people.

With a strong background as a Lawyer, Program Manager, and Community-Based Participatory Research (CBPR) Practitioner, she has participated in international and local development networks—including the Knowledge for Change (K4C) CBPR training fellowship—and leads BCRE’s advocacy on child protection, juvenile justice, menstrual dignity, gender equality, and educational access across Northern Nigeria.

Our Core Programme Areas

BCRE delivers integrated, community-driven programmes across three interconnected pillars and six cross-cutting themes.

Pillar A

Child Protection & Education

BCRE works to promote children’s rights, safe and supportive learning environments, awareness of child protection, and equitable access to quality education.

  • School & community child-rights sensitisation
  • Back-to-school material & uniform support
  • Safe learning spaces & safeguarding education
  • Juvenile justice & stakeholder engagement

BCRE supports women’s and girls' dignity, agency, and socioeconomic opportunities through practical skills, health education, and community inclusion.

Child Protection, Safeguarding & Ethics

Our Commitment to Child Safety, Dignity & Accountability

At Binta Child Rights & Empowerment Foundation (BCRE), the safety, dignity, and confidentiality of every child, girl, woman, and community member come first.

Informed Consent & Ethical Media

We use only respectful, age-appropriate, and consent-based photography and stories. We never publish a child’s full name, school, or personal details where doing so could create a safeguarding risk.

Survivor & Beneficiary Confidentiality

BCRE strictly protects survivor identities and confidential case information. No unapproved beneficiary data or sensitive personal records are shared publicly.

Safeguarding Reporting Pathway

To report a child-protection or safeguarding concern, or to request BCRE’s safeguarding policy framework, contact our designated channel at Bintachildrightempowerment@gmail.com.
