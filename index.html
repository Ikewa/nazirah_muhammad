Who We Are

A Grassroots Movement for Child Rights, Girls’ Education & Women’s Dignity

Binta Child Rights & Empowerment Foundation (BCRE) is a non-governmental, nonprofit organisation founded in 2021 and registered with Nigeria’s Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) in 2025 (RC 8506854).

Operating across Northern Nigeria (Kano) with activities and partnerships extending to Abuja (FCT), we are not here merely to talk about social challenges—we work hand-in-hand with schools, families, traditional leaders, volunteers, and partner institutions to create safe environments where every child can learn and every girl and woman can thrive.

Our Mission

To advance child rights, protection and access to education; promote the safety, dignity and empowerment of women and girls; and equip young people through participation, mentorship, advocacy, skills development and community-led action.

Our Vision

A society where every child is protected and able to learn, every girl can realise her potential, women are empowered, and young people participate meaningfully in shaping their communities.

Our Core Values

Child Safety & Dignity Equality & Inclusion Integrity & Accountability Community Participation Empowerment & Leadership Collaboration Respect & Confidentiality
What We Do

We Don’t Just Talk About Change. We Build It, Piece by Piece.

BCRE runs three interconnected programme pillars supporting children, adolescent schoolgirls, women entrepreneurs, and youth leaders at critical stages of life.

01

Child Protection & Education

Promoting children’s rights, safe and supportive classrooms, school and community sensitisation, education material support, and juvenile justice advocacy.

  • School & community child-rights awareness
  • Back-to-School uniforms, books & learning kits
  • Child safeguarding & stakeholder engagement
02

Women & Girls’ Empowerment

Supporting women’s dignity, agency, and economic independence through menstrual health education, reusable-pad production, and local enterprise skills.

  • FlowS Menstrual Dignity & hygiene workshops
  • WomenWorks skills (pads, skincare, bags, spices)
  • Safe spaces & GBV prevention dialogues
03

Youth-Led Empowerment

Strengthening young people’s leadership, public speaking, mentorship, and civic voice so youth actively shape education and community development.

  • Best Bloomers Debate & Girl-Child celebration
  • Youth mentorship & K4C participatory research
  • Engaging boys & young men in anti-stigma action

Our Child Protection, Safeguarding & Privacy Commitment

At BCRE Foundation, child safety, dignity, and survivor confidentiality are non-negotiable. We use only respectful, consent-based stories and never publish a child’s identifiable details, school, or location where doing so creates a safeguarding risk. To report a safeguarding concern or request our policies, contact Bintachildrightempowerment@gmail.com.
